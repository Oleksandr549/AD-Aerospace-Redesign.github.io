eVTOL Cameras

4K camera for Advanced Air Mobility (AAM).

Bespoke external and internal video for the next generation of rotorcraft and eVTOL airframes.

Built for Advanced Air Mobility

Our eVTOL cameras feature a configurable frame rate up to 60fps and 4K UHD resolution. The optics are protected behind a sapphire-glass window, with an option for an integrated heater.

We provide bespoke, aerodynamically shaped externally-mounted 4K cameras, and light, compact internal cameras for cabin monitoring — with housings that can be designed and adapted to any specific airframe.

Bespoke housings, mounts and integrations are available on request; contact us to discuss bespoke solutions for your airframe.

Use cases

  • In-Flight Entertainment (IFE), incl. streaming to passenger mobile devices
  • Internal cabin surveillance
  • Pilot situational awareness
  • Vertiport approach & landing
  • Automation
  • Ground manoeuvring
