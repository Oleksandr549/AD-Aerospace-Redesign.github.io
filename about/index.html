About Us

Pioneers of aircraft video surveillance technology.

The safety & security of every aircraft we equip

Headquartered in Hazel Grove, Cheshire, UK, AD Aerospace are the pioneers of aircraft video surveillance technology, serving the civil aircraft sector for over 30 years with solutions including flight deck entry, cabin and cargo surveillance, taxi aid and IFE systems.

EASA Parts 21 & 145, FAA and AS9100 Rev D certification head an impressive list of accreditations. AD Aerospace is a Key Vendor to Airbus, Boeing, COMAC and Leonardo, and sole-source supplier to several major aircraft OEM programmes globally — a privilege taken very seriously.

Whether the requirement is catalogue-offerable line-fit or a retrofit installation utilising our unparalleled library of STCs, there is an AD Aerospace solution to suit most major passenger aircraft — with presence in the USA, Europe, Asia Pacific, Middle East and Africa to help wherever the requirement may be.
