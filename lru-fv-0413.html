FV-0413 Internal Aerospace Video Camera

High-sensitivity monochrome or colour dome camera for discreet cabin-overhead mounting, functional to 0.01 lux.

Overview

The FV-0413 Internal Aerospace Video Camera is a high sensitivity monochrome or colour camera and may be mounted discreetly in the cabin overhead using colour co-ordinated housings.

It operates at extremely low light levels, functional to 0.01 lux, substantially lower than the 1.0 lux night-time aircraft cabin lighting SAE requirement. Integrated infrared illuminators enable operation in very low light and reduce backlit silhouetting from window light. The standard field of view is 90°, with other options available.

Key features

  • High-sensitivity monochrome or colour options
  • Discreet overhead cabin mounting with colour-coordinated housings
  • Integrated IR illuminators
  • Functional to 0.01 lux
  • Multiple field-of-view options (90° standard)
