FV-0410 Internal Aerospace Camera

Internal camera for security, passenger monitoring, crew training and flight safety, in overt or covert housings.

Overview

The FV-0410 is specially designed to be fitted to the interior of aircraft. It serves security, passenger monitoring, crew training and flight safety functions.

Monochrome and colour variants are available in NTSC and PAL formats, with digital megapixel options. Housings are offered in overt or covert designs and are compatible with security system integration.

Key features

  • Applications include security, passenger monitoring, crew training and flight instrument recording
  • Monochrome or colour, NTSC or PAL, digital megapixel options
  • Overt and covert housing variants
  • Integrates with security systems
  • Recording capability for analysis
