Flight Deck (Retro-Fit)

Overview

CDSS is a retro-fit camera, switch, monitor and control-panel kit that lets pilots identify anyone requesting Flight Deck access, supplied with EASA or FAA STC approval. It is available via Minor Change and/or STC for B737 NG, B737 MAX, A320 CEO & NEO Family (including A321) and Dash 8 Q400 (other aircraft types available on request).

A retro-fit system installed post aircraft delivery, CDSS satisfies the requirement for a non-intrusive, non-distracting method of identification by the Captain and First Officer of who is trying to gain access to the Flight Deck. It acts as a deterrent to terrorism and air rage and helps to prevent catastrophic loss of aircraft from terrorism or revenge motivation. The system represents a low-cost option to significantly increase levels of aircraft safety and security.
